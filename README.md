# Business Performance Analytics | Power BI

## Project Overview
Developed an interactive Power BI business intelligence solution for a hardware company to analyze business performance across multiple functional areas.
The project includes dedicated analytical views for **Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive Management**, providing stakeholders with interactive KPIs and visual insights for performance monitoring and decision-making.

##  Business Objective
The objective of this project is to transform raw business data into an interactive analytical solution that enables stakeholders to:
* Monitor key business performance indicators
* Analyze financial and sales performance
* Evaluate marketing performance
* Monitor supply chain performance
* Identify trends and areas requiring attention
* Support data-driven business decisions

## Tools & Technologies
* **Microsoft Power BI**
* **SQL** – Data source
* **Power Query** – Data transformation and preparation
* **DAX** – Measures and business calculations
* **Data Modeling** – Relationships between business entities
* **Data Visualization** – Interactive dashboards and KPI reporting

##  Dashboard Views

### Finance View
Provides insights into financial performance through relevant KPIs, trends, and financial analysis.

### Sales View
Analyzes sales performance across relevant business dimensions to identify trends and performance drivers.

### Marketing View
Provides insights into marketing performance and helps evaluate business trends related to marketing activities.

### Supply Chain View
Analyzes supply chain performance and highlights areas that may require operational attention.

### Executive View
Provides a consolidated high-level overview of key business KPIs to support executive-level decision-making.

##  Project Workflow

**SQL Data → Power BI → Power Query → Data Model → DAX Measures → Interactive Dashboards → Business Insights**

##  Key Features
* Multi-page interactive Power BI report
* Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive views
* KPI-driven performance analysis
* Interactive filters and cross-filtering
* Data modeling and table relationships
* DAX-based calculations and measures
* Executive-level business overview
